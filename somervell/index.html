Somervell County Watch

Somervell County is home to the Comanche Peak nuclear plant and Dinosaur Valley State Park. After approving incentives for an Amazon project, its commissioners changed course and voted unanimously to oppose new data centers until the Legislature acts.

Lessons for other counties

  • A county can change course

    Somervell approved incentives, then took a public stand once residents spoke up.

  • Watch for power-plant magnets

    Nuclear and gas plants draw data centers looking for direct power. Neighbors of any big plant should watch for co-location deals.

  • Protect public lands

    A state park nearby gives the whole state a stake. Park users can be allies.
