El Paso County Watch

Meta is building a data center campus in Northeast El Paso, near the state line, now described as a $10B, 3.1-million-square-foot buildout. It is one of the few Texas projects with a public water cap, and residents are now pushing for a community benefits agreement.

The projects

  • Under constructionReporting

    Meta El Paso

    Closed-loop cooling. El Paso Water caps use at 2.5 million gallons a day and expects an average of about 480,000, rising on hot days. Earlier reporting described a 1.5 million gallon maximum. The city's incentive gives an 80% tax cut for 25 years tied to at least 50 permanent jobs; Meta has said it will employ about 300. Land was bought through Wurldwide LLC.

What has happened

  • Late 2023

    Incentives and water agreement

    Meta negotiated an incentive package for an $800M initial investment and a water supply agreement with El Paso Water. The city sold the land to Wurldwide LLC for about $8.5M.

  • Oct 2025

    Construction announced

  • 2026

    Council orders a community benefits agreement

    Staff were directed to draft one after heavy public comment.

  • May 26, 2026

    No more incentives for hyperscale data centers

    Council voted unanimously to stop recruiting or incentivizing future hyperscale data centers.

  • July 2026

    El Paso Water explains the numbers

    The utility said it will hold Meta to the 2.5 million gallon cap.

  • July 20, 2026

    First Data Center Policy Framework

    Adopted unanimously for future projects. It does not apply to Meta, and the council separately voted to keep Meta's incentive deal.

Lessons for other counties

  • A water cap belongs in the contract

    El Paso has one. But a cap five times the expected average leaves a lot of room. Ask for caps close to real need.

  • Ask who pays for power

    A state utility commissioner raised concern that household customers could subsidize the cost of serving the data center.

  • It's not too late for benefits

    El Paso is negotiating a community benefits agreement after incentives were granted.
