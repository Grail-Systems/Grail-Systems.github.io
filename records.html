How the clock works

Under the Texas Public Information Act, an agency must release records promptly. If it can't within 10 business days, it must certify in writing the date the records will be ready. If it wants to withhold anything, it must ask the Attorney General for a ruling within 10 business days.

Our policy: when an agency misses a deadline, we contact it by business day 10, send a written warning by day 15, and go up the chain by day 20. Past-due status stays public unless we've agreed to an extension.
