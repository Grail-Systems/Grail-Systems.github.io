      About this data

      • Members are from the 89th Legislature (2025)

        The November 3, 2026 election may change some seats before the 2027 session. District numbers do not change. Confirm current members at the Texas Legislature's "Who Represents Me" tool.

      • Split counties

        Where a county is split among several districts, its projects are listed under each of those districts until exact locations are confirmed.

      • Senate District 22

        The Legislative Reference Library's county table does not list Senate District 22, which covers counties including Hood, Somervell, Hill, Bosque and McLennan. The seat may be vacant. We show the district without a name rather than guess.

      • Bowie County

        The source table does not list Bowie County's House district. Check "Who Represents Me" for it.

      Sources: Texas Legislative Reference Library, Texas Representatives by county and Texas Senators by county, 89th Legislature (2025). Projects: Texas Watch, with sources on each project.
