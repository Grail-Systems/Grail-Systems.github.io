💧 Our Water. Their Vote. Nov. 18.

East Texas’s four groundwater districts are scheduled to vote on the official limits for how far our aquifers are allowed to drop through 2080, and send them to the state.

Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026 · 10:00 AM
Room 119 (Commissioners Room), Nacogdoches City Hall
202 E. Pilar, Nacogdoches · Official notice

What’s being decided

Groundwater Management Area 11 is Neches & Trinity Valleys, Panola County, Pineywoods and Rusk County groundwater districts. Item 8 on the agenda is a resolution adopting “desired future conditions,” the limit on how far underground water levels may fall, and submitting it to the Texas Water Development Board.

The proposal on the table since April 28 is based on Scenario 26.1. For the Carrizo-Wilcox aquifer, it allows an average drop of up to:

43 ft
Angelina County
50 ft
Nacogdoches County

Average drawdown from 2013 to 2080. Source: GMA 11 proposed desired future conditions, April 28, 2026.

Why it matters now

At its Nov. 9 meeting, the Pineywoods board voted to recommend switching to an older standard called “Scenario 33,” according to a recording of the meeting. By the board’s own account, the stronger standard costs Angelina and Nacogdoches counties next to nothing. There’s no local reason to weaken it.

And the vote is at 10 AM on a weekday, when most of the people who live on this water are at work.
