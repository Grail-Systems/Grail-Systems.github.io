Ellis County Watch

South of Dallas, Red Oak and Midlothian have become one of the densest data center clusters in Texas, with Google, DataBank and Compass all building, and thousands more acres bought for future campuses.

The projects

  • Under constructionRecords (TDLR)

    DataBank Red Oak campus

    292 acres off Stainback and Batchler roads, up to eight buildings and 480 MW. Each building is filed under its own LLC, such as DB Data Center Red Oak 12, LLC. State TDLR filings list building DFW14 at 3600 Batchler Rd at $325M, starting Dec 1, 2026.

  • Under constructionRecords (TDLR)

    Compass Red Oak campus

    375 acres around Austin Blvd, about 360 MW. TDLR lists Building 6 at 500 Austin Blvd at $100M. Proposed a $15M grant for a county animal shelter in June 2026 amid opposition.

  • OperatingReporting

    Google Midlothian campus

    On Railport Parkway, built starting in 2019 through a shell company, Alamo Mission LLC, under the project name Sharka. 375 acres plus 165 more; Phase 4 under construction.

  • OperatingReporting

    Google Red Oak campus

    About 165 acres. First building operating. Part of Google's $40B Texas plan through 2027.

  • Land acquiredReporting, verify

    Cawley Partners data center park

    About 5,200 acres south of Dallas, acquired in 2025.

Lessons for other counties

  • State filings show buildings before they rise

    TDLR's TABS database listed DataBank's $325M building with an exact address and start date. Anyone can search it by county.

  • Shell names hide the owner

    Google built here as Alamo Mission LLC. Watch land and permit records for unfamiliar LLCs.

  • Get gifts in writing

    A $15M grant offer is not a contract. Benefits belong in a binding agreement.

  • Count the whole cluster

    One city can host many campuses. Ask for a study of their combined water and power use.
