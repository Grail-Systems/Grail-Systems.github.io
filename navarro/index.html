Navarro County Watch

South of Dallas, Corsicana hosts one of the largest bitcoin mining campuses in the world, now shifting to AI, and more developers are lining up behind it. The water comes from Navarro Mills Lake.

The projects

  • Operating and expandingReporting

    Riot Corsicana

    Riot Platforms' campus, which ERCOT approved for 1 GW, the first 1 GW load it approved. About 400 MW is built on 858 acres. AMD leases 50 MW for AI computing. The City of Corsicana said in 2022 that Riot's water would come from Navarro Mills Lake; Riot has never published how much.

What has happened

  • 2022

    Riot breaks ground

    Residents formed a Facebook group of more than 500 members and started a petition against the mine.

  • 2025

    Riot buys more land

    The campus grows to 858 acres.

  • 2026

    AI moves in

    AMD doubles its lease at Corsicana to 50 MW.

Lessons for other counties

  • Crypto mines become AI campuses

    The same site, power and water can switch uses. Agreements should require a new hearing for any change in use.

  • Ask the lake's owner

    Navarro Mills Lake water is contracted through the Trinity River Authority. Drought restrictions have been triggered before.

  • Watch what follows

    Once one big load arrives, powered-land developers follow.
