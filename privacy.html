Grail Systems

Privacy Policy

Effective Nov. 26, 2026

Grail Systems builds public records tools for the East Texas Legacy Coalition (ETLC) and for other groups, newsrooms and attorneys. This policy covers grailsystems.io, easttexaslegacy.org and the pages under them.

1. What we collect

  • Landowner and neighbor sign-ups: your name, email, phone if you give it, the general location and size of your land, your well, whether you own your water or mineral rights, and anything else you choose to tell us.
  • Emails and messages you send us: whatever is in them.
  • Paying clients: the contact and billing details needed to do the work and send invoices.
  • We don't ask for Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or ID numbers. Please don't send them.

2. How we use it

  • To contact you about the issue you signed up for, only as often as you asked.
  • To organize landowners and prepare petitions, comments or records requests. Nothing is ever filed with your name on it without your OK.
  • To answer you, and to do the work a client hired us for.

3. Who we share it with

  • We never sell your personal data, including sensitive data such as precise location, and we never share it with developers or the companies we track.
  • We share a person's name, email or location with an allied group or attorney only after that person says yes in writing.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it, such as a court order. If that happens, we'll tell you unless the law forbids it.
  • Our sites don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't run analytics that follow you around the web.

4. What's public

Our maps, county pages and records logs are built from public records. They name public officials, agencies, companies and public filings. They don't name private residents without permission.

5. How we keep it

Sign-up forms send your answers to a private, password-protected Google spreadsheet that only Grail Systems and ETLC can open. Our websites are hosted on GitHub Pages. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep private data off our public sites. If your information is ever exposed in a security breach, we'll tell you as Texas law requires.

6. Your choices

  • Ask what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time.
  • Stop updates at any time by replying "stop" or emailing us.
  • Write to contact@grailsystems.io or PO Box 668, Huntington, TX 75949. We answer within 10 business days.

7. Children

Our tools are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new date at the top. Big changes that affect sign-ups get an email.
