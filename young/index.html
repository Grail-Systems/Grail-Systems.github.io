Young County Watch

Near Graham, a developer has proposed one of the largest private investments in Young County's history: a 15-building data center campus called Project Saltworks. Residents want to know where the water will come from.

The projects

    Project Saltworks

    Stream Data Centers. About 867 to 890 acres in unincorporated Young County, south of FM 61 and north of FM 209, near Graham. Up to 15 buildings and about $10B reported.

What has happened

  • June 22, 2026

    Residents confront the developer

    A crowd told commissioners and the developer the campus would take the county's water and change its way of life. Commissioners took no vote.

  • July 2026

    Plans reported by industry press

    15 buildings and about $10B in investment.

  • Sept 2026

    State freezes data center permits

    The Governor told TCEQ to pause data center permits until state grid and water audits are done.

Lessons for other counties

  • Ask for the water source in writing

    No numbers have been made public. Ask the developer, the county and any water supplier.

  • Raise chemicals early

    Residents asked about PFAS. Ask which chemicals a cooling system will use and where its wastewater goes.

  • Use the state pause

    With state permits paused, there is no reason for local officials to rush.
