A local board said no

Most wins started the same way: neighbors read the agenda, showed up, and gave a city council, a zoning commission or a county court a public reason to vote no.

College Station, Brazos County

The city wouldn’t sell the land.

Priority Power wanted about 200 acres of city land for “Project Aggie.” Thousands signed a petition, nearly 80 people spoke, and on Sept. 11, 2025, the council voted unanimously against the sale.

KXXV · BCS Chronicle

San Marcos, Hays County

A rezoning denied, then a ban.

Hundreds packed City Hall in February 2026 and the council denied the rezoning. In June, San Marcos became the first Texas city to bar data centers from every zoning district.

KSAT · Texas Tribune

Killeen, Bell County

A 4 to 0 vote, then a withdrawal.

Residents raised noise and environmental concerns about a $30 million data center on South Fort Hood Street. Planning and Zoning voted 4 to 0 on April 27, 2026, to recommend denial, and the applicant withdrew.

Texas Scorecard · KXXV

Tyler, Smith County

No permit for a bitcoin mine.

Planning and Zoning denied Vulcan Core’s special use permit 5 to 2 on July 7, 2026. A city board rejected its appeal over a missed deadline; a legal fight continues.

Data Center Dynamics · Smith County

Wichita Falls

Who’s the end user?

The council voted unanimously in May 2026 against rezoning land beside homes for a data center whose operator was never named.

Know your tools, No. 1 · Wichita County

Prosper, Denton County

Eight precincts objected.

The planning commission recommended denial 7 to 0 for “Project Tomahawk,” and the developer withdrew in September 2026.

Denton County

Somervell County

Not next to the dinosaur tracks.

The county voted down a 450-acre data center beside Dinosaur Valley State Park on May 11, 2026, and resolved to oppose new data centers until the state acts.

Somervell County

The paperwork tools

Some wins came from a form most people never hear about.

Hutto, Williamson County

A written zoning protest.

Neighbors filed two formal written protests against rezoning land for a data center. A week later, in April 2026, the developer withdrew. How it works

FOX 7 Austin

Sulphur Springs, Hopkins County

A deed restriction.

The coal mine land the city planned to use came with a deed barring power generation. A judge granted an injunction, and the city appealed.

Texas Sixth Court of Appeals, No. 06-26-00025-CV

Water said no

Data centers need water on a scale small towns rarely see. When the water provider holds the line, the project often can’t go on.

Fort Spunky, Hood County

Not enough water.

Hood County commissioners rejected Pacifico Energy’s concept plan 3 to 2, citing insufficient water. Stopped for now; residents are still watching it.

Texas Scorecard · Hood County

Cedar Creek Lake, Henderson County

A water pause, a petition, then a withdrawal.

The local water district paused large new commitments in May 2026. Thousands signed a petition, and on July 23 Diode Ventures withdrew after falling short of the governor’s standards.

Office of the Governor · Texas Tribune

Pauses and bans

Cities have more power here than counties, because Texas counties generally can’t zone.

Athens, Henderson County

An emergency moratorium.

The City Council adopted a one-year moratorium on data centers, with the option to extend.

Athens Independent

Lubbock

Nearly 4,000 signatures.

A rezoning for an AI data center was scrapped in January 2026. Residents then gathered more than 3,900 verified signatures for a pause. In September the council said it would consider no large data centers until rules are in place, and began the steps toward a 90-day moratorium.

High Plains Public Radio · Texas Public Radio

The hard lessons

Not every “win” is the end. Here’s what we’d want every county to know before it celebrates.

Hood County

They can move next door.

Amazon told 60 Minutes it decided not to build in Hood County after asking for up to 90% in tax rebates for 30 years. CBS reported the project is moving forward in the next county over, where Amazon got the rebate it wanted.

CBS News

Harlingen, Cameron County

City limits stop at the city line.

Harlingen set a 120-day moratorium. Fluidstack started building just outside city limits, out of its reach.

Know your tools, No. 3

Hill County

A county pause, sued away.

Hill County passed what appears to be the first Texas county moratorium, then rescinded it two weeks later after a developer sued for $100 million.

Texas Tribune

Hood County

A denial isn’t the end.

Commissioners rejected a Comanche Circle settlement and denied the developer’s permit appeal on Oct. 13, 2026. The developer warned it could sue. We still show the project as approved.

Texas Public Radio

Fort Worth

A moratorium takes months.

The council started the legal process on Aug. 11, 2026. The first public hearing was Nov. 10; the second hearing and first reading are set for Jan. 12, 2027. Until then, nothing is paused.

City of Fort Worth

Crystal City, Zavala County

Ask who pays if they go broke.

A bitcoin mine in a leased warehouse lost half its capacity in a fire, and the company filed for bankruptcy in February 2026. Ask every developer who cleans up if the company goes under.

Zavala County
