Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it here, in public, with the date. Facts move fast, and so do these projects. If you see an error, email contact@grailsystems.io.

Log

  • Nov 1, 2026

    Williamson County AI Campus

    We listed this as an announced project with the developer not identified, pinned near Georgetown. It is Skybox and Prologis's PowerCampus Austin in Hutto, and buildings are already built and under construction. Developer, status and pin are fixed.

  • Nov 1, 2026

    KDC Project Comal

    We placed Project Comal near Georgetown and showed it as only announced. It is in Taylor, at County Road 401 near the Samsung plant, and the city annexed the land for it in March 2026. The pin has been moved and the status changed to approved.

  • Nov 1, 2026

    CloudHQ map pin

    The pin for CloudHQ's San Antonio campus sat about 25 miles east of the real site. The campus is on Lambda Drive in the Texas Research Park, on the west side, across from Microsoft. The pin has been moved.

  • Nov 1, 2026

    Wichita Falls Business Park

    We named only a shell company, "Texas Data Center Campus LLC," and listed 800 MW. The developer is Skybox Datacenters, buying through Texas Data Centers Campus LLC. Skybox markets the campus at 300+ MW; the card now shows both figures. Groundbreaking has been pushed to early 2027 after the state audit.

  • Nov 1, 2026

    Prosper data center

    We showed the Prosper data center along FM 1385 as still filed, with the developer not identified. The developer was White Rose Partners, and it withdrew Project Tomahawk in September 2026 after the planning commission recommended denial 7 to 0. Updated to stopped.

  • Nov 1, 2026

    San Marcos: one project, two cards

    The map listed Highlander SM One (San Marcos) and the San Marcos data center on Francis Harris Lane as separate projects. They are the same proposal. Both cards now say so.

  • Nov 1, 2026

    Denton: likely one site, two cards

    The map shows Core Scientific Denton and a CoreWeave Denton AI data center as separate projects. Reporting indicates CoreWeave is the tenant at Core Scientific's Denton site, so these are likely the same place. Both cards now say so, and the 394 MW figure on the CoreWeave card is marked unconfirmed.

  • Nov 1, 2026

    Google's shell companies in Ellis County

    We listed Alamo Mission LLC as the company behind Google's Midlothian campus. State building filings tie Midlothian to Sharka LLC, and Alamo Mission LLC is tied to Google's Red Oak campus, both in the Comptroller's records and in a 2020 water filing. Moved to the right cards, with sources.

  • Nov 1, 2026

    Tyler bitcoin mine

    We said the City of Tyler denied a rezoning and that the developer was not identified. It was the Planning and Zoning Commission, voting 5 to 2 on Vulcan Core LLC's special use permit request, and KLTV reports a legal fight over the denial continues. Corrected, with sources linked.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    Texas water reporting

    Several pages said Texas does not require data centers to report water use. That was true until this fall. In September 2026 the Governor directed the Texas Water Development Board to enforce mandatory reporting; the results are not yet public. Updated on the Texas Watch, Water and handout pages.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    Bon Wier, Newton County

    We first listed a signed purchase agreement for a data center campus here, based on the owner's July 31 press release. The owner's CEO later told KJAS that no sale or deal was made. The entry now shows both statements and is marked disputed.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    El Paso "300-foot buffer"

    We first listed a 300-foot buffer rule for El Paso. That figure matches Fort Worth's proposed rules, not El Paso's. El Paso's actual actions, a vote to stop recruiting hyperscale data centers and a new policy framework, now appear instead.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    Fort Spunky status

    Listed as paused. Later reporting describes it as canceled. Updated.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    Amazon in Hood County

    Listed as planned at 2300 Coates Road. Reporting identifies it as Project Spectrum, about $5B on 1,265 acres, now withdrawn. Updated.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    Project Patriot

    First listed with few details. Updated to show the developer, Bilateral Energy LLC, the 2,100-acre Knox Ranch site, Granbury's annexation and the residents' lawsuit.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    Longleaf power plants in Angelina County

    The map first showed one 600 MW Longleaf plant. ERCOT lists two separate 600 MW entries, so Longleaf II was added. Whether both will be built is not yet known.

  • Oct 31, 2026

    Hood County project sources

    Several Hood projects first came from an industry tracker alone. They were checked against news reports and updated where the reports differed.
