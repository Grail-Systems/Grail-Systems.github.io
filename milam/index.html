Milam County Watch

In Rockdale, the land where Alcoa once smelted aluminum now hosts two of the largest bitcoin mines in the country, side by side. AI tenants are moving in, and OpenAI's Stargate has named Milam County as a site.

The projects

  • OperatingReporting

    Riot Rockdale

    Riot Platforms, 700 MW, on former Alcoa land. Riot bought about 200 acres it had leased for $96M and signed AMD as an AI tenant. Uses a mix of air and immersion cooling, with a lake water reservation reported at about $2.1M a year.

  • OperatingReporting

    Bitdeer Rockdale

    Spun off from China's Bitmain, less than half a mile from Riot on former Alcoa land.

  • AnnouncedReporting

    Stargate Milam County

    Named as an OpenAI Stargate site in late 2025. Details are still not public.

Lessons for other counties

  • Old industrial land draws new loads

    The Alcoa site had the power lines; the miners followed. Compare Lufkin's paper mill and Bon Wier's plywood mill.

  • Ask who owns the water rights

    A private lake water reservation can bypass the debates residents expect.

  • Track every announced site

    Stargate named Milam County with no public details. Watch TDLR filings and the appraisal district.
