Grail Systems

Public records, put where people can see them.

Grail Systems builds tools that pull government data into one place, so a community can see who’s deciding what, who’s paying for it, and what it’s costing them. Government records are public. They just aren’t always easy to find. We fix the second part.

Designed and built by Fawn Elizabeth Zion, systems architect, Lufkin, Texas.

Aquifer Defense Grid

Built for the East Texas Legacy Coalition

Data centers and power plants are lining up for water and power in Deep East Texas, right on top of the Carrizo-Wilcox Aquifer the whole region drinks from. The Grid puts every project, permit, water level and campaign donation on one public map, and every point links back to the record it came from.

Sources: ERCOT, EPA ECHO, the Texas Water Development Board, the Texas Ethics Commission, LegiScan and Texas Legislature Online, the Angelina County Appraisal District, and public information requests.
